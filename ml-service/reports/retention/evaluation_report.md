## Threshold selection (validation data only)

- operational = argmax F2 on validation; high = lowest threshold above it with validation precision >= 0.75.
- Operational threshold **0.18**: validation recall 0.910, precision 0.477, flags 49.7% of participants.
- High-risk threshold **0.39**: validation precision 0.750.
- Risk levels: `low` below the operational threshold, `medium` between the two, `high` at or above the high threshold.

### False negatives vs false positives

A false negative is an at-risk participant the screen misses and who therefore gets no extra support; a false positive is a participant who receives an unnecessary (cheap, non-clinical) check-in. Because support actions are low-cost and the cost of losing a participant is high, the operational threshold weights recall twice as much as precision (F2) instead of using 0.5. The price is a larger flagged group and lower precision; compare the 0.5 and operational rows above. Change `f_beta` in `config.py` to move the trade-off.
